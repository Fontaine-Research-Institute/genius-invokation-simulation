## Effect

> When the character uses a Normal Attack: Physical DMG dealt by the character is converted to Hydro DMG. If the character is in the Ousia state, heal all of your characters on standby for 1 HP. If the character is in the Pneuma state, deal +2 DMG for this instance, but deal 1 Piercing DMG to your character who has taken the least DMG.
