## Effect

> When clockwork meka that have either Arkhe: Ousia or Arkhe: Pneuma receive DMG aligned with the opposite Arkhe, a pneuma-ousia annihilation reaction will occur, and they will become Deactivated.
> Deactivated clockwork meka will not be able to use their original abilities, and can only use Deactivated abilities. Deactivated abilities cannot deal Elemental DMG, and can only deal Physical DMG.
