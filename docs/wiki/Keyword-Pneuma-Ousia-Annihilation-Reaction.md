## Effect

> After an Ousia-aligned character takes Arkhe: Pneuma-aligned DMG, or after Pneuma-aligned characters take Arkhe: Ousia-aligned DMG, a Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation Reaction will occur.
> Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation reactions will have different effects on different types of cards.
